Contact: support@klix.nc · Hours: Monday–Friday, business hours in New Caledonia time (UTC+11) · Languages: English, French

Severity Example First response
Critical App blocks work item creation or edits wrong data within 24 hours
Normal A rule does not apply as expected within 1 business day
Question / request "Can it do X?" within 2 business days

Before writing to us: open the app's admin page → Activity log and include the work item key, the channel used (Create button, backlog, API, email, portal) and a screenshot of the rule.

Known limits: fields required at creation are only covered by the Create dialog; the compact Create view is covered after creation; priority is not replaced after creation (use the project's priority scheme); completion after creation takes a few seconds (up to a few minutes during Atlassian incidents); "External System Import" (CSV) is not covered; changes appear in the history under the app's name.
